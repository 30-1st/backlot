Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Backlot is a social media consultancy app for X. It is run by Kymarli Fisher, a sole proprietor based in New Jersey, United States ("Backlot", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information Backlot collects, how it is used, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

The short version. We use your information only to run Backlot for you. We never sell it, never use X data for advertising, and never use it to train AI models. Backlot never posts, replies, likes, follows or sends messages for anyone. Screenshots you show Xev are read once and never stored, and when you connect your X account, your direct messages are read only to alert you and draft replies, and their words are not stored.

1. Information we collect

  • Your login: a username and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider).
  • What you give us to run your account: your brand kit (name, niche, offers, links, goals, tone settings), notes, inspiration board items, posting decisions, the wins you log, and messages in your team chat.
  • Photos and videos you upload: location and device details are removed on your device before upload.
  • Public X data: through X's official API we read public profiles, public posts and public post counts for your account and your niche. We do not scrape X.
  • Screenshots you show Xev: names and photos are blurred on your device first. The image is sent once to our AI provider, read, and not stored anywhere by Backlot.
  • If you connect your X account: with your permission through X's own sign-in, Backlot can read your direct messages to spot people interested in buying and to draft replies for you to send. Backlot cannot send messages. We do not store the text of your messages; we keep only the conversation's identifier, where it stands, and a short private summary, for up to 30 days. You can disconnect at any time in Backlot or in your X settings.
  • Basic technical data: what your browser needs to load the app, and limited request logs kept by our hosting providers for security.

2. How we use it

  • To plan, research and write posts with you, coach your sales conversations, report how your account is doing, and keep your team chat working.
  • To keep Backlot safe: bot checks on public pages, spending limits, and preventing abuse.
  • To check and improve the quality of our AI agents. For accounts that agreed to it, we save some real chat moments as private test cases, visible only to Backlot's operator.

We do not sell your information, do not use it for advertising, and do not use X data or your content to train AI models. We do not profile individuals or infer sensitive traits about anyone.

4. How long we keep it

  • Team chat messages: 30 days.
  • Xev's notes on a sales conversation: 30 days after the last update.
  • Screenshots: not kept at all.
  • Excerpts of public X posts: removed within 24 hours of being removed from X, and refreshed or cleared within 30 days.
  • Photos and videos you remove: kept 7 days so you can restore them, then deleted.
  • Your account and brand kit: for as long as you use Backlot. Logins left unused for 60 days, other than paying clients', are deleted with their content.
  • Private quality test cases (only for accounts that agreed): until they are switched off or you ask us to delete them.

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete your information, and to withdraw any consent you gave (including disconnecting X or opting out of quality test cases). Depending on where you live, laws such as the New Jersey Data Privacy Act or the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you these rights; we honor these requests for everyone. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Database access is limited by row-level rules so each person sees only what they are allowed to, sign-in tokens and secret keys are kept on the server only, and screenshots are blurred on your device. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected users promptly if a breach affects their information.

7. Age and adult content

Backlot is for people 18 and older. Some clients work in adult niches; that content is handled for adults only, is never shown to minors, and is never used to make images of real people.

8. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date at the top. For significant changes we will tell you in the app.
